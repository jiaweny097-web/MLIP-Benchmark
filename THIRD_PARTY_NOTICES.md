# Third-party provenance

The repository-level noncommercial grants cover original contributions only.
They do not replace the permissions attached to these sources:

- Materials Project: Jain et al., https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4812323.
  Material IDs and original dataset fields are retained. Verify the terms applying
  to the actual downloaded records and acquisition/API version.
- Surface/work-function dataset: Schindler et al.,
  https://doi.org/10.1002/adfm.202401764. Preserve the original record identifiers.
  Confirm the actual archive/download URL, dataset version and license.
- High-entropy alloy source: Li et al., https://doi.org/10.1039/D4TA00982G.
  Confirm the source archive license and retained derived-subset permissions.
- Tensile reference curves: literature references33–36 in the manuscript.
  The supplied curves are reference data, not newly calculated DFT in this repository.
  Add exact per-element acquisition/digitization notes and source citations.
- Pretrained checkpoints: the six projects in metadata/models.csv. Software
  licenses may differ from checkpoint/data licenses; check the specific artifact
  before redistribution. The local model directory is ignored until review.
- VASP POTCAR/binaries are not distributed. For DFT reruns, provide potential
  identifiers and settings without redistributing licensed pseudopotentials.

Unconfirmed permissions above are release-preparation items, not a declaration
that they have already been cleared.
